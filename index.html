Current Endeavors: Navigating the Software Development Horizon

At Pratishthan Software Ventures Pvt. Ltd, I currently work as a Team Lead, leading a team of developers while designing and building enterprise payment solutions. I have contributed to the development of payment systems including NACH, SEPA (EBRT1, EBSDD, EBSCT), BOOK, QCBRTP, and UAERTP, as well as ACH payment products.

I played a key role in developing the Finacle OE Paymentsbackend for the Hong Kong Faster Payment System (HKFPS) and implemented the Foreign Exchange module for Payments. I also optimized system performance by introducing bulk database inserts, improving transaction processing across Transaction Handler, inbound, and outbound microservices.

My expertise includes backend engineering, distributed systems, payment processing, microservices, REST APIs, RabbitMQ, Node.js, Redis, MongoDB, Docker, Git, performance optimization, and scalable FinTech solutions.
